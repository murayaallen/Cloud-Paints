The flier's product descriptions live in `client-package/build/prices.js` as
`desc`. Two groups are not the client's words:

- **Four were never sourced:** Transil-Stone Guard, NC Sanding Sealer, Black
  Bituminous, Road Marking Thinner.
- **Three were expanded on 2026-09-09** to fill the full-width rows on the
  flier's back panel: Super Gloss Premium, Floor Paint, Universal Undercoat.
  They describe what each product does and where it is used and claim no
  performance figure, but they say considerably more than the originals did.

**Why:** these print as statements of fact about products we do not make.

**How to apply:** flag them for the client before any press run. They have not
been signed off.
