01

Modern Concrete.

Architectural calm. A matte concrete read that masks minor wall imperfections while pulling apartments, TV walls and reception areas into a quietly modern voice. Perfect for modern apartments, industrial interiors, TV walls and office reception areas.

02

Decorative Pattern.

Pattern, applied with hand and tool. Combs, rollers, trowels and texture brushes lay irregular character that reads as artisan, not factory. Perfect for accent walls, bedrooms, luxury lounges and restaurants.

03

Venetian Plaster & Luxury Marble.

High-end finish with depth and movement that changes under different lighting. Layered, burnished and finished by hand. Perfect for luxury homes, hotel lobbies, master bedrooms and executive offices.

04

Stone & Exterior Rock.

Durable, weather-resistant decorative textures with a natural stone appearance. The collection that makes boundary walls, gate pillars and commercial façades read as architecture instead of plaster. Perfect for exterior walls, boundary walls, gate pillars and commercial buildings.

05

Metallic & Shimmer.

Real metallic particles in a water-based binder — trowel-applied for a brushed-metal shimmer that catches light all day. Premium decorative effect for the single feature wall that anchors the room. Perfect for feature walls, luxury lounges, hotel interiors and showrooms.

How we apply it

Three site visits. One finished wall.

01

Consultation

A site visit, a finish sample, a colour direction. We confirm substrate readiness, suggest the family that suits the room, and quote.

02

Surface prep

Plaster cures, walls are sanded and sealed. Texture is unforgiving on poor substrates, so this step is where the finish is won.

03

Hand application

Our certified decorative team lays the texture — trowel, sponge, comb, brush — in the agreed family and tone. Walls are finished and protected.
