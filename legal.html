Performance & Analytics Cookies: help us understand how visitors use our website — number of visitors, pages most visited, time on site, navigation patterns.

Marketing & Advertising Cookies (if applicable): show relevant product advertisements, measure marketing campaign effectiveness, track engagement with promotional content.

4.6 Managing Cookies

4.7 Data Collected Through Cookies
