Built for real weather. Built for real homes.

Kenya’s climate is demanding — intense sun, heavy rains, humidity, dust and heat all attack your walls every day. That’s why we developed Dual Protection Technology Paints: a next-generation weather-proof paint system designed to protect your surfaces from both external and internal damage factors.

What is Dual Protection Technology?

It is a high-performance coating system that combines two layers of protection in one solution.

1. External weather shield

Protects your walls from:

  • Heavy rainfall and water penetration
  • UV radiation and sun fading
  • Dust accumulation and pollution
  • Surface erosion and cracking

This layer acts like a raincoat for your walls — strong, sealed, and durable.

2. Internal surface-strengthening layer

Works beneath the surface to:

  • Bind paint more strongly to the wall
  • Reduce peeling and flaking over time
  • Improve adhesion on rough or porous surfaces
  • Stabilise the wall surface for long-term durability

This is the foundation layer that keeps your finish intact for years.
