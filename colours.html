    Matte / Eggshell

    Ceilings, low-traffic walls, bedrooms. Hides surface flaws; softest look.

    Satin / Silk

    Living rooms, hallways, kids’ rooms. Gentle glow, wipeable, everyday durable.

    Semi-Gloss

    Kitchens, bathrooms, doors, trim. Moisture-resistant and easy to scrub.

    High-Gloss

    Furniture, feature doors, metal. Hard, mirror-like, maximum protection.

    Textured

    Feature walls & exteriors. Limewash, suede, sandstone & concrete effects.
